    Why stock does not oversell

    1. Check the order key and fingerprint inside a transaction.
    2. Insert the unique order, then conditionally decrement each product only if stock ≥ quantity.
    3. Commit everything together, or abort everything. No application lock.

    The CLI probes race 20 and 100 buyers against five units and force an abort after the first reservation. The dashboard itself does not expose a failure switch or reset route. Payments are not implemented; database idempotency is not exactly-once execution of external side effects.
